Why AgriQueue?

Revolutionary queue management for government procurement centres

Hybrid Access

Online + Kiosk tokens in one unified queue. No farmer is left behind due to digital exclusion.

Fair Queue Algorithm

Alternating O-K-O-K token allocation ensures equitable access for both online and kiosk farmers.

Real-Time Visibility

Live queue position and ETA help farmers plan their time efficiently instead of waiting blindly.

Physical Centre Automation

Digital display and voice announcements ensure no farmer misses their turn, even without smartphones.

End-to-End Transparency

Track quality check, weight measurement, procurement amount and payment status in real-time.

Operational Analytics

Centre administrators get real-time insights to optimize capacity, reduce wait times and improve efficiency.
